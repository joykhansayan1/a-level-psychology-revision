Common questions

I paid but features are locked

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account (or, on Android, Google account) used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → select the subscription → Cancel. Or use Manage Subscriptions.

On Android: open the Google Play app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → A-Level Psychology Topics → Cancel subscription. Or use Google Play subscriptions.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone, iPad) or through Google Play (Android).

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Questions and progress are stored on your device. An internet connection is needed for App Store or Google Play purchases and restore.

Is this an official AQA/Edexcel/OCR product?

No. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with any exam board.
